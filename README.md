# India's Local Trains

Suburban rail passengers a day in India, and Mumbai's local network with animated trains.

- Passengers: Indian Railways Annual Statistical Statements 2024-25 (suburban passengers originating ÷ 365).
- Tracks & stations: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- Train dots are illustrative; their density follows ridership.
